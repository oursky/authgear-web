## Overview

Palace Studios is transforming Hong Kong’s fitness landscape by providing freelance trainers and wellness practitioners with flexible, tech-enabled private studios. Their mobile app allows trainers and clients to book premium, fully-furnished spaces by the hour for personalized sessions, all in central city locations.

To streamline user access, Palace Studios integrated Authgear’s phone number OTP authentication, eliminating passwords and boosting both security and convenience. Authgear’s platform also enabled Palace Studios to offer a unified, seamless login experience across both mobile and web, ensuring effortless account access for all users
