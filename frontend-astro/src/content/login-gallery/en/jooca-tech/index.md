## Overview

星際毛毛 is a dedicated app for pet owners, serving as both a comprehensive pet diary and a convenient service booking assistant. Users can easily record their pets’ health data, daily behaviors, and veterinarian notes, ensuring all important information is organized in one place. Beyond health tracking, the app allows owners to search for and book a variety of local pet services, making it simple to keep their furry companions happy and well cared for.

To accelerate development and provide a seamless user experience on both Android and iOS, 星際毛毛 integrated Authgear’s authentication solution. With support for passwordless login via SMS, as well as one-tap sign-in with Apple and Google, Authgear enables fast, secure, and user-friendly access. This not only sped up the app’s launch but also ensures pet owners can manage their pets’ needs effortlessly and securely.
