## Overview

WorkKing is Hong Kong’s leading platform for connecting employers with short-term workers across roles like temporary waiters/waitress, housekeepers, promoters, and shop attendants. With dedicated iOS and Android apps for both employers and job seekers, WorkKing streamlines the hiring process, allowing employers to post jobs and workers to quickly find flexible gigs in a secure, user-friendly environment.

To enhance security and simplify access, WorkKing uses Authgear’s passwordless SMS login, eliminating the need for traditional passwords. Authgear also verifies both email and phone numbers, ensuring a smooth, trustworthy experience for all users while safeguarding sensitive data and supporting WorkKing’s rapid, reliable job-matching service
