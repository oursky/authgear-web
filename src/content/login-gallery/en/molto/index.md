## Overview

Molto is a developer-friendly analytics SaaS tool designed for engineering teams seeking actionable insights with minimal friction. To deliver a seamless yet secure onboarding experience, Molto leverages Authgear’s authentication suite—supporting Password, Email Magic Link, LinkedIn, and GitHub logins. Developers can sign up using their preferred identity provider, ensuring quick and convenient access to analytics tools. For enhanced security, Molto also offers two-factor authentication (2FA) with Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP), giving users an extra layer of protection without compromising usability. With Authgear, Molto combines developer-centric convenience with enterprise-grade security, so teams can focus on building and analyzing, not managing credentials.
