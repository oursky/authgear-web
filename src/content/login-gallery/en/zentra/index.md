## Overview

Zentra is a goal-setting and learning tracker app built for students. With features that encourage consistency and provide AI-powered feedback, the platform needed a secure and intuitive account system. Authgear enabled Zentra to streamline student–teacher account separation, implement social login, and enhance trust through email verification.
