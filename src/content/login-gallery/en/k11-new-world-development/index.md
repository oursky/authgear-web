## Overview

K11, a flagship brand under New World Development, is renowned for its luxurious shopping malls, hotels, and office spaces that blend art, culture, and lifestyle. The K11 app serves as a digital gateway for members to access exclusive privileges, event information, and a comprehensive shop directory, reflecting the brand’s commitment to delivering a premium, curated experience for its high-end clientele.

To further enhance the lifestyle ecosystem, New World Development also offers the Artisanal Living app for residents of its properties. This one-stop platform provides property information, lifestyle benefits, and essential services such as maintenance and laundry. Residents can earn and spend K Dollars—NWD’s reward currency—on management fees, clubhouse facilities, and more, seamlessly integrating daily living with luxury rewards.

Authgear powers the unified login experience across the K11, Artisanal Living, and K Dollar apps, ensuring both security and convenience. With single sign-on, multi-factor authentication, email magic links, and SMS OTPs, users enjoy quick and secure access to all platforms. This integration not only protects user data but also strengthens the K11 brand’s promise of effortless, elevated service for its discerning members.
