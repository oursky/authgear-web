## Overview

Elise is a minimalist high-end jewelry brand that values every detail of the online shopping experience. To ensure secure transactions and seamless account access for premium customers, Elise adopted Authgear’s passwordless login and social sign-in features. The result is a secure, elegant login experience that aligns perfectly with the brand’s aesthetic.
