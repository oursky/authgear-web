## A New Era of Sign-Ups and Unified Login: Phone Number OTP and Authgear

Dinesh turned to Authgear, a cutting-edge authentication solution, to address both challenges. By implementing Authgear's phone number One-Time Password (OTP) feature, Dinesh eliminated the need for traditional password-based authentication, enhancing security and convenience for users.  
  
Furthermore, Authgear's platform enabled palace studios to expand beyond the mobile application to websites without compromising user experience. The seamless integration ensured a consistent and unified login experience across all platforms, allowing users to access their accounts effortlessly.

## Security, Speed, and Flexibility Combined

Authgear's phone number OTP offered a powerful combination of security and convenience. New users could simply enter their phone numbers to receive a verification code, bypassing the complexities of creating and remembering passwords. This streamlined process significantly reduced sign-up friction, encouraging more users to join the palace studios community.  
  
Additionally, Authgear's adherence to OpenID Connect (OIDC) standards provided flexibility for palace studios to collaborate with different software vendors without compromising user data security. Dinesh could integrate various platforms and services without sharing sensitive user information, ensuring a secure and reliable ecosystem.
