Bupa replaced Azure AD B2C with Authgear to enable Face/Touch ID for infrequent users—cutting password resets, lowering support costs, and improving satisfaction, with a platform ready for future authentication needs.

## At a glance

- **Industry:** Health insurance & healthcare services
- **Use case:** Reduce password friction for infrequent users
- **Auth methods:** Biometrics (Face/Touch), passwordless options, MFA-ready
- **Outcome:** Fewer password resets and support tickets; faster, easier access

## About Bupa

Bupa is a global healthcare group serving millions of customers through health insurance and a broad range of medical services. With a focus on improving health outcomes and access to care, Bupa partners with providers and invests in digital tools that make care simpler, safer, and more convenient for members.

## The challenge

Bupa serves millions across insurance and medical services. But most members only sign in **once or twice a year**. Combined with **strict, complex password policies**, this led to frequent credential resets and rising support costs. Their existing platform, **Azure AD B2C**, had **limited native biometric support**, making it hard to deliver the frictionless experience users expect.

## The solution

Authgear provided a flexible path to a modern, low‑friction login.

1. **Biometric authentication**   Secure, intuitive Face/Touch ID sign‑in for quick access—ideal for infrequent users.
1. **Seamless migration from Azure AD B2C**   A smooth transition preserved existing accounts and minimized downtime.
1. **Security without the struggle**   Enterprise‑grade controls with a faster, password‑light experience (MFA‑ready, policy‑compliant).

## Results

- **Lower support burden:** Significant reduction in password‑related tickets
- **Higher satisfaction:** Faster, familiar sign‑in via biometrics
- **Better engagement:** Easier access encourages more consistent usage
- **Future‑ready:** Foundation to add new factors and support upcoming use cases

## Why Authgear for healthcare

- **Biometrics first:** Native support for Face/Touch ID
- **Migration friendly:** Azure AD B2C → Authgear with minimal friction
- **Compliance conscious:** Security features aligned to enterprise requirements
