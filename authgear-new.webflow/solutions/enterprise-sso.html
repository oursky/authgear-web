Enterprise SSO Features Built for Real-World Identity

Standards-based SSO with deep directory integration, flexible identity provider support, and full user lifecycle management. Authgear combines enterprise security controls, self-serve experiences, and compliance-ready operations in one platform.
Protocols: SAML & OpenID Connect
Integrate with existing apps based on the SAML and OIDC standard.
Active Directory & Entra ID integration
Connect via SAML, OIDC, or AD FS. Map groups to roles, sync profile attributes, and preserve source-of-truth ownership.
One integration, multiple identity providers
Support different IdPs for different customers or business units in the same project.
User lifecycle & provisioning Choices
Manage users via Admin Portal or API. Sync users with HR systems to maintain single source of truth.
Frontline & contractor access
Securely onboard non-corporate users using personal email/phone. Apply simpler login for high-volume apps while keeping back‑office security strict.
Self‑serve settings & admin portal
End users manage their profiles, passkeys, MFA, sessions, and connected accounts. Admins get search, bulk actions, audit logs, analytics.
Risk & bot protection
CAPTCHA, rate limits, adaptive MFA, and anomaly controls to reduce abuse and SMS pumping risk.
